# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting
responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, sexualized language, personal attacks,
publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `pso9809@gmail.com`. Reports will be
reviewed promptly, fairly, and confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or
reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when necessary.
